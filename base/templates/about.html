About

A calmer way to get things done.

Most task apps try to do everything. This one keeps to the essentials: write it down, check it off, move on. Nothing gets lost, and nothing gets in your way.

01

Focus

One clean list, a quick way to add to it, and a single click to finish. No projects, tags or settings to manage.

02

Forgiveness

Completed tasks are kept as a record, and deleted ones wait in the trash until you decide. Mistakes are always undoable.

03

Privacy

Every list belongs to its own account. Only you can see, change or delete your tasks.

And Stripes. A small assistant who reads your list and reminds you what's still waiting.
